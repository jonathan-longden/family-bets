      About

      The weather is real. Every number on this screen comes straight from the forecast — temperature, what it feels like, humidity, wind, gusts, chance of rain, visibility, sunrise and sunset. The jokes sit on top of those numbers and are chosen by them. If it is grey, nothing here will tell you the sun is out.

      Fifteen days, honestly labelled. The first three days are the detailed ones. Days four to seven are a fair bet. Anything past that is the shape of the weather rather than a promise, and the app says so rather than dressing a guess up as a fact.

      Where it comes from. Open-Meteo, which answers this page directly and wants no key, no account and no email address. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a pair of coordinates.

      No signal? It shows you the last forecast it managed to get, with the time it got it, rather than pretending that is the weather now.
