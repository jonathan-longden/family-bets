        About

        The weather is real. Every number on this screen comes straight from the forecast. The jokes sit on top of those numbers and are chosen by them — if it is grey, nothing here will tell you the sun is out.

        Fifteen days, honestly labelled. High confidence for the first few days, good for the rest of the week, lower after that. The app shows the best forecast available and never pretends day fifteen is tomorrow.

        Where it comes from. Open-Meteo, which answers this page directly and wants no key, no account and no email address. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a pair of coordinates.

        No signal? It shows the last forecast it managed to get, with the time it got it, rather than pretending that is the weather now.
