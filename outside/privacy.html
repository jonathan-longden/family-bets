Privacy

Blooming Weather — the polite name on a weather app with a mouth on it.

Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version. Blooming Weather stores your saved places, settings and cached forecasts on your device. When you use online features, information is sent to the services that provide weather, map tiles, warnings, advertising or purchase verification. The app has no sign-in account or separate analytics SDK, although AdMob may process analytics-related information as described below. On Android, Premium is a £4.79 one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play and removes adverts after our verification Worker confirms the purchase.

Location and saved places

  • If you choose Use my location, the app asks the operating system or browser for a location fix. On Android it requests the coarse-location permission. On iOS it requests location while the app is in use and asks iOS for reduced accuracy by default; iOS settings may affect the accuracy provided. In a browser it requests geolocation with enableHighAccuracy: false, which asks for a lower-accuracy fix but does not guarantee that only coarse location is provided. The app rounds a live location result to four decimal places before saving or using it for a forecast.
  • You can instead search for a town. The search text is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding API. Choosing a result saves that place and coordinates rounded to four decimal places on your device. Places loaded through the app's import feature can retain greater coordinate precision, and the app can use those coordinates as imported.
  • Your saved place names, optional labels, coordinates, selected/startup place, preferences and cached weather data are saved in the app's browser storage on the device. The cache includes the latest forecast and a short previous-forecast summary used to describe changes. The app does not send this entire saved-place list to its weather providers.
  • Location permission is optional. You can search for a place instead. The app requests a location when you use that feature; it does not continuously track your movement.

Weather and place search

The app requests forecasts directly from Open-Meteo at api.open-meteo.com, and town search from geocoding-api.open-meteo.com.

  • Forecast requests include the selected place's latitude and longitude, forecast periods and the weather fields requested by the app. Coordinates from live location and place search are rounded to four decimal places by the app; imported place coordinates may have greater precision.
  • Place searches include the text you entered. Open-Meteo and the other internet services contacted by the app can receive normal connection data such as your IP address and request details.

Storm Tracker, warnings and map

The Storm Tracker map uses the OpenFreeMap style at tiles.openfreemap.org. Loading the map causes the device to request map style and tile resources; the map is centred on the selected place when one is available. OpenFreeMap's service can therefore receive connection data, including your IP address and map tile requests that indicate the displayed area.

Official UK warning data is requested from our Cloudflare Worker at blooming-weather-warnings.longdenjonathan89.workers.dev/api/warnings. The app makes a GET request for the warnings feed without adding your selected coordinates to that request. The feed displays Met Office warning information supplied through that service. The app code does not contact the Met Office directly; how the Worker obtains and handles that feed is governed by the service operator's arrangements.

Advertising and consent

On native devices, the app refreshes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent information at startup. If Premium is already verified at startup, the app still refreshes consent information so the Settings privacy-options status can be shown, but it does not initialise AdMob, show the advertising consent form or request adverts. For users without verified Premium, if UMP reports that consent is required and a form is available, the app presents the advertising consent form and requests adverts only when UMP reports that ads can be requested. If Premium is purchased during an existing session, AdMob may already have been initialised. After the Worker verifies the purchase, the app hides the banner and suppresses further advertising display; it does not unload an AdMob SDK that was already initialised. The web version does not use the native UMP or AdMob flow. A consent form is not necessarily required or shown in every region or session.

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK through the Capacitor AdMob plugin. Its resolved Android Mobile Ads SDK dependency is version 25.4.0. Google's current Android data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK describes the latest SDK as automatically collecting and sharing IP addresses, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. That is Google's SDK-level disclosure; the exact handling in this app may vary with the bundled SDK version, configuration, region, consent and ad use. The app code itself establishes the UMP consent checks above, but does not enumerate every datum handled inside Google's SDKs. Google's current iOS guidance says its Mobile Ads SDK can collect coarse location when the app has location permission, and that automatic coarse-location collection is enabled by default starting with SDK 13.12.0. This app bundles iOS SDK 13.11.0 and does not set the later coarseLocationCollectionEnabled control; Google's current documentation does not establish the exact coarse-location behavior of that bundled version. The app's own ad request code does not add the selected place coordinates. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's broader privacy practices. Verified Premium users do not receive adverts from the app.

In the native app, Settings includes a Privacy options control when UMP reports that one is required, and hides it when UMP reports that it is not required. The control opens Google's UMP privacy-options form. The web version does not use the native UMP controls.

Premium purchases and verification

On Android, Premium is a one-time lifetime purchase offered at £4.79 through Google Play Billing. The app uses the product ID blooming_weather_premium. After Google Play returns a completed purchase, the app sends a JSON POST to our Cloudflare Worker at https://blooming-weather-purchases.longdenjonathan89.workers.dev/verify. The JSON includes productId and the Google Play purchaseToken, plus available transaction details (platform, transactionId, orderId, receipt and jwsRepresentation). Empty/unavailable details are sent as null. The Worker is responsible for checking the purchase with Google Play.

The app unlocks Premium only after the Worker returns a successful response that positively marks the purchase as verified, valid or premium. Purchase tokens and the associated request are therefore processed by the Worker and Google Play for verification; those services also receive normal connection information such as IP address. On app startup and when you select Restore Purchases, the app checks purchases available to the Google Play account on the device and sends eligible Android purchases for verification, so a purchase can be restored on another Android device using the purchasing Google Play account. This Google Play purchase verification and restoration flow applies to Android. Google Play may also receive purchase and acknowledgement requests from the app. The app does not unlock Premium from a local preference alone.

Other device storage and sharing

The app keeps its settings and cached information in local browser storage used by the app, and uses temporary device cache storage when preparing a share image. The share card contains the selected place name, weather and app name. When you choose Share, the app hands the card and/or text to your device's share sheet; the destination you choose then handles it under its own privacy terms. The app does not include a separate analytics SDK. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process interaction and diagnostic information, including for analytics; the purposes and data described vary by platform and SDK version, as set out in Google's disclosures above.

Deleting local data

Settings → Erase everything clears the app's saved places, settings and cached forecasts from this device. Deleting the app or clearing its site data also removes local app data. These actions do not themselves delete information already processed by Google, Open-Meteo, OpenFreeMap, Google Play, or the Cloudflare services. Their retention and deletion practices are governed by their own policies and service arrangements.

Children, changes and contact

The app has no account system. It contains strong language when the swearing setting is on; this can be turned off in Settings. If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

Questions about this policy can be raised by email at weatherblooming@gmail.com, or through the project's repository.
