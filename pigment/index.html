Colour by number

Make your own

Turn a photo already on this device into a picture to colour in. It's worked out here on the phone — the photo is never uploaded anywhere.

How it works

  • Every square carries a number. Pick that number from the row of colours and the square is yours — squares waiting for the colour you're holding are tinted, so they're easy to find.
  • Drag to fill a run of squares in one go. Squares with a different number politely ignore you.
  • Pinch to zoom, or use two fingers to move around. Double tap to jump in close and back out again.
  • Finish a colour and the next one is handed to you automatically. Lost? Hint points at a square that's waiting.
  • Your progress is kept on this device, picture by picture, so you can put it down mid-sky and come back to it.

Works offline — add it to your home screen and it opens with no signal at all.
