      What goes in

      Amounts in pounds. Draw and loss are £0 unless you want a trophy that fills whatever happens.

      Bank link

      A web page cannot reach into a bank account on its own. Give it the address of an automation that can — an IFTTT, Zapier or Make webhook wired to your bank's "move money into a pot" action — and every win fires the stake down it. Leave it empty and the trophy just keeps score.

      The noise it makes

      The cannon is made up on the spot by the phone, so it needs no download and works with no signal. "My own sound" plays an audio file you already have — a song you own a copy of, say. It is kept on this phone and goes nowhere; trim it to the bit you want first, since only the first 45 seconds are played. A sound can only be played while the app is open, and on some phones only after you have tapped the screen once.

      Scores

      The free test key works for a bit of use. If results stop coming, put your own key here (thesportsdb.com). Results are never trusted while a match is still on — a win counts once the match is finished.

      The trophy itself

      Everything lives on this phone and nowhere else. Nothing is backed up for you, so export before you change phones.

      How the money moves

      Every finished Arsenal match is read from a public results feed. A win adds £10 to the trophy — once, and only once, however many times you open the app.

      The trophy keeps score. That is all it does on its own. It shows what has gone in, what you have not moved yet, and what you have taken back out.

      The bank link moves the money. A page in a browser has no way into your bank, and any app that claims otherwise is really talking to something else that does. So this one talks to something else that does: a webhook you own. Wire an IFTTT, Zapier or Make webhook to your bank's own "deposit into a savings pot" action, paste the address into Settings, and each win fires the tenner down it with the match on the reference. Every fire carries the match id, so a repeat cannot pay twice.

      Nothing else leaves the phone. No account, no server of mine, no bank credentials here — the webhook address is the only thing this app knows, and you can pull it out at any time.
