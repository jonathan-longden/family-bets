Neon Chomp

Eat every pellet. Dodge four ghosts. Take your corners tight.

Arrows / WASD to move · P to pause

How to play

  • Move with the arrow keys, WASD, a swipe, or the on-screen pad. Line up your turns early — a buffered turn fires the moment the gap opens.
  • Chain pellets by eating them back to back. Keep the chain alive and every pellet is worth up to five times as much.
  • Power pellets in the corners turn the ghosts blue. Eating them in one power-up scores 200, 400, 800, then 1600.
  • Fruit appears near the ghost house twice a level and is worth a lot more as the levels climb.
  • The ghosts get meaner. On level 1 they're slower than you and take their time leaving the house. Every level they close the gap, hunt for longer and stay scared for less, until they're on your heels.

Runs entirely offline — add it to your home screen and it works with no signal.
