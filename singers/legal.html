Terms of use

Last updated: [date]. Spotlight is run by [your name or company], contact [contact email].

Who can use it

  • You must be 13 or older. If you are under 18, you confirm a parent or guardian knows you use it.
  • Scout accounts are for adults working in the music industry and are granted by us, by invite code or after review. We can withdraw scout access at any time.
  • One account per person. Don't impersonate anyone.

What you post

  • You keep ownership of your clips. You give us permission to store them and show them to other people using Spotlight, so the app can work. You can delete a clip at any time, which removes it for everyone.
  • Only post recordings you made. If you're singing someone else's song, that's a cover — mark it honestly and don't claim it as an original.
  • Don't post: anything sexual, violent or hateful; anything that isn't a singing performance; anyone else's recording; or anyone who hasn't agreed to appear.
  • We can remove any clip and close any account, and we don't have to explain in advance where someone's safety is involved.

Contact details and scouts

If you're 18 or over you can add a contact email or link, which verified scouts see when they shortlist you. Under-18 accounts cannot share contact details, and scouts are told to approach through a parent or guardian. We do not check what a scout does after that — deal with people the way you'd deal with any stranger offering work, and involve an adult you trust.

No promises

Spotlight is provided as-is. We don't promise it will be available, that your clips are backed up, or that anyone will discover you. Keep your own copies of anything you care about.

Privacy

What's stored, and where

  • On your device only: your birth year, the notes scouts write, saved searches, blocked accounts, and every video file you record.
  • Shared with everyone using this Spotlight (if sync is switched on): your name, handle, bio, city, genres, whether you're over or under 18, your clips and their captions, likes, comments, follows, and — for adults only — the contact details you chose to add.
  • Video files are uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage when sync is on, so other people can play them. Anyone with the link can play a clip.

What we don't do

No advertising trackers, no analytics SDKs, no selling data to anyone. Firebase (Google) processes what's synced, as our hosting provider.

Your choices

  • Delete any clip from your profile, which removes it everywhere.
  • Settings → Reset erases everything about you on that device.
  • Email [contact email] to have your account and clips removed from the shared board.

Copyright

Covers

Singing someone else's song and posting it is a use of their work. Spotlight doesn't hold licences for the songs people cover; a rights holder can ask us to remove a clip and we will.

Reporting infringement

Email [contact email] with: the clip (a link or the singer's handle), what it infringes, proof you hold or represent the rights, your contact details, and a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised. We remove clips on credible complaints and tell the person who posted it.

If your clip was removed

Reply to the notice explaining why you have the right to post it. If it was a mistake we'll put it back.
