    Tuner is a player, not a service. It carries no channels of its own — it plays the playlist you already pay for, straight from your device.

    Playlists

      Free to watch

      Channels their broadcasters publish openly — no subscription, nothing to sign in to. Catalogued by the iptv-org project, fetched straight from there. Good for trying the app out.

      Your own playlist

      Paste the M3U link your provider gave you. It's fetched straight from your device and saved here.

      Paste the contents of an M3U playlist.

      Xtream Codes details, if that's what your provider uses. Tuner builds the M3U and guide links from them.

      Settings

      TV guide

      An XMLTV link shows what's on now and next. Most providers have one; Tuner fills it in automatically when the playlist names it.

      This device

      Clear

      Removes every playlist, favourite and saved guide from this device. Nothing was ever anywhere else.

      Keep it on your phone

      iPhone: tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

      Android: tap the browser menu, then Install app.

      It then opens full screen like any other app, with your playlist and favourites already there.
